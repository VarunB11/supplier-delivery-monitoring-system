Supplier Performance Evaluation

Transparent, mathematical scorecards based on verified delivery track records

Standardized Procurement Reliability Formulas (Academic Rigor & Industry Standards)
1. On-Time Delivery Rate:
(On-Time Completed / Eligible Completed) × 100
Excludes cancelled & active pending orders.
2. Average Delivery Delay:
Sum of positive delay days / Eligible Completed
Evaluates severity of late deliveries.
3. Quantity Fulfilment Rate:
(Total Accepted Qty / Total Ordered Qty) × 100
Measures quantity conformity across orders.
4. Composite Score & Rating:
70% (On-Time %) + 30% (Quantity Fulfilment %)
>=90% Excellent | >=75% Good | >=60% Avg | <60% Needs Imp.
